The visualizations directory contains all figures generated throughout the temporal‑geometry study. These images represent the final, interpretable form of the computational pipeline and serve as the bridge between raw entropy data and the scientific conclusions presented in the paper. Every visualization in this folder is produced directly from the analysis scripts and reflects the temporal behavior uncovered across different entropy modalities and manifold resolutions. Together, these figures illustrate how entropy moves, bends, drifts, oscillates, and settles into basins and attractors over time.

The images here include manifold‑comparison figures, curvature plots, oscillation diagrams, basin and attractor illustrations, drift‑hierarchy renderings, flow‑mapping diagrams, and raw temporal‑dynamics plots. They also include the TT20 drift visualizations that demonstrate real‑time temporal‑geometry behavior captured through the Android probe. Each visualization corresponds to a specific dataset and analysis module, and the complete mapping between probes, datasets, and analysis scripts is documented in docs/probe_dataset_pipeline_mapping.md. This ensures that every figure can be traced back to the exact computational steps that produced it.

These visualizations are not decorative; they are the primary evidence of temporal geometry. They reveal the structure that statistical randomness alone cannot show, including persistent oscillation across encodings, curvature emerging from entropy flow, basin formation within symbolic and numeric manifolds, and attractor behavior that remains stable across multiple resolutions. The figures in this directory are the same ones referenced throughout the paper and are essential for understanding the temporal‑geometry framework as a whole.
