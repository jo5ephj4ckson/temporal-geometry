The supplementary materials provide additional documentation, figures, and methodological detail that support the results presented in the main manuscript. These materials include extended temporal‑geometry visualizations, expanded manifold comparisons, additional drift‑hierarchy plots, and high‑resolution curvature and basin analyses that were not included in the main text due to space constraints. The supplementary figures illustrate the same temporal‑geometry behaviors observed in the primary results, including oscillation, curvature, basin formation, attractor stability, and resolution‑independent drift patterns across multiple entropy modalities.

The supplementary materials also include detailed descriptions of the probes used in the study, the structure of the datasets hosted on Zenodo, and the complete probe‑dataset‑pipeline mapping that links each entropy source to its corresponding analysis modules and visual outputs. These documents provide deeper context for the temporal‑geometry framework and allow researchers to trace every result back to its originating entropy stream. Additional reproducibility notes are included to clarify how the analysis scripts operate, how manifold encodings are generated, and how temporal‑geometry metrics are computed across symbolic, numeric, and raw manifolds.

All supplementary figures, documents, and extended analyses are available in the visualizations and docs directories of the GitHub repository associated with this study. The full dataset archive required to reproduce the supplementary results is hosted on Zenodo under the DOI referenced in the main manuscript. Together, these materials provide a complete and transparent extension of the main paper, ensuring that every aspect of the temporal‑geometry study can be independently verified and explored.
