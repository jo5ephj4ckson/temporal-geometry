1. Bill of Materials (BOM)
LM358P op‑amp — dual, single‑supply, DIP‑8 package

LED probe — standard 5 mm LED, any color; forward voltage 1.8–3.2 V depending on color

Bias resistor — 330 Ω, ¼ W, ±5%

Decoupling capacitor — 0.1 µF ceramic, placed across VCC/GND

USB 5 V supply — standard USB‑A port

3.5 mm TRS jack — panel‑mount, tip/ring/sleeve

Sabrent USB audio adapter — model AU‑MMSA or equivalent

General wiring — 22–26 AWG solid or stranded hookup wire

2. Op‑Amp Pin‑Level Wiring (LM358P)
Pin 8 (VCC): connect to USB +5 V

Pin 4 (GND): connect to USB ground

Pin 3 (Non‑inverting input): LED probe output node

Pin 2 (Inverting input): feedback from Pin 1

Pin 1 (Output): amplified signal → TRS jack tip

Optional: second op‑amp channel unused (Pins 5–7)

3. LED Probe Biasing
LED anode → 330 Ω resistor → USB +5 V

LED cathode → ground

Probe output node is taken directly from the LED anode (the junction between LED and resistor).

No additional filtering or buffering is required.

4. Output Interface
TRS jack tip: LM358P output (Pin 1)

TRS jack sleeve: ground

TRS ring: unused

Connect TRS jack to Sabrent AU‑MMSA line‑in.

5. Physical Layout Notes
Keep LED probe leads short (<5 cm) to reduce parasitic capacitance.

Place the 0.1 µF decoupling capacitor as close as possible to Pins 4 and 8.

No shielding or enclosure is required for basic operation.

6. Operating Conditions
Supply voltage: 5 V USB

Typical LED current: 8–12 mA

Op‑amp output amplitude: 0.1–1.2 V depending on probe behavior

Sampling: 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz via Sabrent AU‑MMSA
